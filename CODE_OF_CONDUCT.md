# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient (many people here are musicians, not programmers), stay on topic, assume good faith, and no harassment, insults or personal attacks.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, report it privately through [GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-repository-responsibly/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads or block participants who break these rules.
